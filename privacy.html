Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

iIntroduction

Welcome to File to PDF. This Privacy Policy explains what information our app handles and why. Your trust matters to us — the app is designed to work entirely on your device, so your files stay in your hands.

1Your Files Stay on Your Device

File to PDF converts images and text documents into PDF files. All of this processing happens locally on your phone. The PDFs you create are saved to your device's storage. We do not upload your images, text files, or generated PDFs to any server.

2Information We Collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect or store your name, email address, or any files you convert. The only information that may be collected is used strictly for standard app services:

  • Advertising: To show ads through Google AdMob, the service may use identifiers such as the Advertising ID to provide relevant ads and measure performance.
  • Analytics & Crash Reporting: We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. These services may receive technical data such as device model, operating system version, and crash logs.

3How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.
  • To display ads and measure advertising performance.
  • To diagnose and fix issues through crash reporting and analytics.

4Advertising

The app is free and supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to serve ads. Google may use the Advertising ID to serve personalized ads. In regions that require it (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google provides a consent screen before personalized ads are shown.

You can learn how Google uses advertising data at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5Third-Party Services

To provide these services, the following third parties may process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (by Google) — analytics and crash reporting

Please review their policies for details on how they handle data.

6Data Retention & Security

Your files remain on your device and are never transmitted, so there is nothing for us to store. Technical usage data processed by the third-party services above is retained according to each service's own policies. We take reasonable steps to keep app data secure, and we do not combine it with personal identifiers from any other source.

7Permissions

File to PDF does not need and does not request any special permissions from your device. For its core features it uses only Android's built-in system capabilities, which require no app permissions:

  • Picking images or text files — through the system's own file picker.
  • Taking a photo — by opening your device's camera app.
  • Saving PDFs — using the system's standard save feature.

The app connects to the internet only to load advertisements (AdMob) and to send anonymized crash reports.

8Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can delete it.

9Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
